- Secure remote access & VPN  
- Automation for application deployment (AppMan)  
- Real-time alerting, watchdog monitoring and telnet health checks  
- Payment systems architectures & PCI-DSS aware environments  

I'm passionate about **automation, observability, AI-assisted operations,  
Web3, decentralized applications, and building products that scale**.

I balance hands-on engineering with long-term architectural thinking  
and I enjoy designing systems that are reliable, secure, and measurable.

If you would like to collaborate on DevOps, SRE, AI systems, fintech, or distributed platforms, I would love to connect.
